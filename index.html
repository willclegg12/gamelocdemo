Game Localization Demo

CN→EN Naming & Localization Showcase
游戏本地化测试 — 中译英命名与翻译示例
William Clegg LinkedIn

A localization showcase built around Aniimo (伊莫), a creature-collection action RPG by FunPlus. This demo covers terminology, dialogue, promotional copy, story, fictional events, organizations and place names — demonstrating how a Chinese-language game world can be transposed into natural, compelling English while preserving brand identity and cultural resonance.

以趣加旗下的生物收集动作RPG《伊莫》为基础的本地化展示案例。涵盖术语、台词、PV文案、剧情、虚构节日、组织及地名等命名示例，展示如何将中文游戏世界转化为自然、有吸引力的英文，同时保留品牌识别度和文化内涵。

伊莫 Aniimo The game's collectible creatures. A coined portmanteau blending "animal" + "imo" (from the Japanese 芋, suggesting something round and cute). The doubled "ii" is a deliberate stylistic choice for visual distinctiveness and mirrors the game's playful tone.
游戏中的可收集生物。由"animal"+"imo"（源自日语芋，暗示圆润可爱之物）合成的新造词。双写"ii"是刻意的视觉设计，呼应游戏的活泼基调。		 Invariant — never "Imos" or "Imo." Plural is also "Aniimo" (zero plural, like "sheep" or "Pokémon").
形态不变——不可写作"Imos"或"Imo"。复数同样为"Aniimo"（零复数，如"sheep"）。
谜立方 Aniipod The capture device. Blends "Aniimo" + "pod," evoking a small, self-contained capsule. The "ii" mirrors the creature name, creating brand cohesion. Avoids "ball" (too close to a competitor franchise) and "cube" (too literal from 立方).
捕获装置。由"Aniimo"+"pod"合成，唤起小巧自足胶囊的联想。"ii"与生物名称呼应，形成品牌统一感。避免使用"ball"（过于接近竞品）和"cube"（过于直译）。		 Capitalized. Tiered variants: Aniipod Pro (超级立方), Aniipod Mega (究极立方), Aniipod Hyper (冠军立方).
大写。分级后缀：Aniipod Pro、Aniipod Mega、Aniipod Hyper。
威力 Might A core combat stat. "Might" was selected over "Power" or "Strength" to avoid collision with other RPG stat names and to give the game its own tonal identity — slightly archaic, matching the fantasy-adventure register.
核心战斗属性。选用"Might"而非"Power"或"Strength"，避免与其他RPG属性名冲突，赋予游戏自身的语调特色——略带古风，匹配奇幻冒险的文体风格。		 Capitalized when referring to the stat. Lowercase "might" in general prose.
作为属性名时大写。普通行文中小写。

2. Dialogue & Lines

台词

Register calibration across character archetypes 不同角色语域校准

Tutorial NPC — Dr. Lewis / 莱维导师
这就是联结的力量！通过与伊莫建立联结，你能借助它们的元素之力战斗！
This is the power of Twine! By forming a Twine with your Aniimo, you can channel their elemental power in battle!
Rationale 翻译说明
Dr. Lewis is a mentor figure — warm, authoritative, explanatory. The translation maintains an instructional register with controlled enthusiasm. Key terms "Twine" and "Aniimo" are integrated naturally into the sentence flow. "Channel" elevates the tone beyond the literal "borrow/use."
莱维导师为引导型角色——温和、权威、富有说明性。译文保持教学语域和适度的热情。关键术语自然融入句式流畅度中。"Channel"提升了语调，超越了直译的"借助/使用"。
Playful / Cutesy Character 活泼/可爱角色
嘿嘿~伊莫们今天也很有精神呢~
Hehe~ The Aniimo are full of energy today too~
Rationale 翻译说明
Tildes (~) are retained as a deliberate stylistic choice for characters with a playful, childlike register. This is a project-specific convention — tildes signal a character's personality type in the CN source, and removing them would flatten the characterization.
保留波浪号(~)是针对活泼、童真语域角色的刻意风格选择。这是项目特定惯例——波浪号在中文原文中标示角色性格类型，删除会使角色刻画趋于平淡。
System / UI String 系统/界面文本
确定消耗星愿币切换伊莫的闪耀光彩吗？
Are you sure you want to spend Starwish Coins to switch this Aniimo's Sparkling variant?
Rationale 翻译说明
UI confirmation strings require absolute clarity — no ambiguity about the action or cost. "Spend" is more natural than "consume" for a currency context. Every game term (Starwish Coin, Aniimo, Sparkling) rendered exactly per the locked termbase with no paraphrase.
UI确认文本要求绝对清晰——操作和代价不得有歧义。"Spend"在货币语境中比"consume"更自然。所有游戏术语严格按照锁定术语库翻译，不做改写。

3. PV / Promotional Copy

PV文案
Perfume Item Description 香水物品描述
萦思之语——每一缕香气，都是未曾说出口的思念。
Whisper of Longing — every wisp of fragrance is a longing left unspoken.
Naming rationale 命名依据
The perfume name 萦思之语 (lit. "words of lingering thoughts") was rendered as "Whisper of Longing" — "whisper" captures the intimacy and delicacy of fragrance, while "longing" distills 萦思 into a single evocative English word. The alliterative echo of "longing...left...unspoken" gives the line lyrical quality suited to luxury item marketing. Fragrance naming in EN markets favors abstract emotional states (cf. Chanel's "Chance," Dior's "Joy").
香水名"萦思之语"译为"Whisper of Longing"——"whisper"捕捉了香气的亲密与细腻，"longing"将"萦思"凝练为一个富有感染力的英文词。"longing...left...unspoken"的头韵呼应赋予文案适合奢侈品营销的诗意品质。英文市场的香水命名倾向于抽象情感意象。
Event Banner Copy 活动横幅文案
金秋良夜，盈月相邀，满载一季丰收的喜悦，与你共赴这场月华下的欢愉集会！
On this golden autumn night, the full moon invites you — bringing a season's worth of harvest joy — to a festive gathering under the moonlight!
Rationale 翻译说明
Promotional copy demands rhythm and imagery. The CN piles up four-character phrases (金秋良夜, 盈月相邀); the EN restructures into a single flowing sentence that preserves the harvest-moon imagery while reading naturally. "A season's worth" is an idiomatic rendering of 满载一季 that avoids the stilted "fully loaded with a season of."
宣传文案讲究节奏和意象。中文叠用四字短语；英文重组为一个流畅的长句，保留丰收月色的意象同时确保自然阅读感。"A season's worth"是满载一季的地道译法，避免了生硬的直译。

4. Story & Plot

剧情

World-building terminology 世界观术语

CN EN Naming Rationale & Cultural Context
命名依据与文化语境
神灵 Aeon The game's divine beings. "Aeon" (from Greek αἰών, "age/epoch") conveys timelessness and cosmic scale without defaulting to "God/Deity," which would carry unwanted real-world religious connotations. Familiar to JRPG/fantasy audiences while remaining distinct within Aniimo's mythology.
游戏中的神圣存在。"Aeon"（源自希腊语αἰών，意为"时代/纪元"）传达永恒与宇宙尺度，避免使用带有现实宗教色彩的"God/Deity"。JRPG/奇幻玩家群体熟悉该词，同时在Aniimo的神话体系中保持独特性。
始祖神 Prime Aeon The supreme deity. "Prime" (first, original) + "Aeon" creates a clear hierarchy within the divine naming system. Avoids "Supreme God" or "Ancestral God" — too literal, too generic.
至高神灵。"Prime"（首位、原初）+"Aeon"在神灵命名体系中构建清晰层级。避免过于直译和通用的"Supreme God"或"Ancestral God"。
神使 / 使徒 Guardian Divine emissaries. "Guardian" was chosen over "Apostle" (too Christian-coded) or "Herald" (too passive). Communicates protective purpose and active agency, fitting their in-game role as powerful defenders.
神圣使者。选择"Guardian"而非"Apostle"（基督教色彩过浓）或"Herald"（过于被动）。传达守护意图和主动性，契合其游戏内强力守卫者的角色定位。

Plot-relevant dialogue 剧情关键对话

World Lore Narration 世界观叙事
这片大地曾经是神灵的乐园……直到那场灾变改变了一切。
This land was once the Aeons' paradise... until the Cataclysm changed everything.
Rationale 翻译说明
"The Cataclysm" (capitalized, with definite article) signals a specific, named event in the game's lore — a standard fantasy-fiction convention that immediately tells the player this is a pivotal backstory event. The ellipsis creates a dramatic pause matching the CN's tone.
"The Cataclysm"（大写，带定冠词）标示游戏世界观中的特定命名事件——这是奇幻小说的标准惯例，让玩家立刻意识到这是关键的背景事件。省略号营造与中文原文一致的戏剧性停顿。

Seasonal Event Copy 赛季活动文案

Cultural note 文化说明
This event draws on Mid-Autumn Festival imagery (full moon, harvest, gathering) without naming the real-world holiday — a common localization strategy that preserves cultural flavor while keeping the game's world self-contained. The EN preserves the moon/harvest imagery without needing to explain the cultural reference, as these are cross-culturally resonant symbols.
该活动借用了中秋节意象（满月、丰收、团聚），但未点明现实节日——这是一种常见的本地化策略，保留文化韵味的同时维持游戏世界的自洽性。英文保留了月亮/丰收意象，无需解释文化背景，因为这些是跨文化共通的符号。

前期区域。将诗意的中文（风吻="风之亲吻"）归化为一目了然的表达。"Breezy"保留了风的联想同时感觉亲切——适合新手区域。		 Standard proper noun capitalization.

沿海区域。"Whisperwake"为合成新词："whisper"（呼应"潮音"，海潮之声）+"wake"（波浪留下的尾迹，兼有"觉醒"之意）。打造独特、令人印象深刻的奇幻地名。		 Never "Whisperwake Islands" — "Isles" is part of the established name.

粉团蜂 Puffby 粉团 (powder/puff ball) + 蜂 (bee) → "Puff" + "-by" (a diminutive suffix echoing "bee"). Cute, round-sounding, immediately communicates "fluffy bee creature." The "-by" suffix establishes a naming pattern for the bee family.
粉团（粉扑球）+蜂 → "Puff"+"-by"（模拟"bee"的后缀变体）。发音圆润可爱，立即传达"毛茸茸的蜜蜂生物"。"-by"后缀为蜜蜂家族建立命名规律。
蜜冠蜂 Royalby 蜜冠 (honey crown) → "Royal" (crown association) + "-by" (bee-family suffix). The shared suffix creates a visible evolution line — players can intuit that Puffby evolves into Royalby.
蜜冠（蜂蜜王冠）→ "Royal"（王冠联想）+"-by"（蜜蜂家族后缀）。共同后缀构建可辨识的进化链——玩家可以直觉地理解Puffby进化为Royalby。
霜锋狼 Glynsera A coined fantasy name. "Glyn" (Welsh: valley/glen, evoking cold mountain terrain) + "sera" (suggesting a sleek, fierce creature). The wolf family uses distinct coined names where each evolution sounds progressively more powerful: Emberpup → Flameruff → Scorchhowl (fire); Bonesky → Fenrier → Glynsera (ice).
虚构的奇幻名称。"Glyn"（威尔士语：山谷，唤起寒冷山地地形）+"sera"（暗示流线型凶猛生物）。狼系使用独特造词，每个进化阶段听起来逐步增强力量感。

Summary: Localization Principles

总结：本地化原则
  • Consistency over creativity — Once a term is locked, it is used identically across all contexts: UI, dialogue, tooltips, promotional material. No synonyms, no paraphrases. 一致性优先于创造性——术语一旦锁定，在所有语境中统一使用：UI、对话、提示、宣传材料。不使用同义词，不做改写。
  • Register matching — Tutorial NPCs speak clearly; playful characters keep their quirks; UI text prioritizes zero-ambiguity clarity; PV copy aims for rhythm and imagery. 语域匹配——教程NPC表达清晰；活泼角色保留其特色；UI文本优先确保零歧义的清晰度；PV文案追求节奏和意象。
  • Cultural transposition over literal translation — Place names, creature names and event names are reimagined to feel native in English while preserving the CN source's intent and associations. 文化移植优先于直译——地名、生物名和活动名经过重新构思，在英文中呈现出本土感的同时保留中文原文的意图和联想。
  • Systematic naming families — Related creatures share suffixes or patterns so players can intuit relationships: -by for bees, -eetle for beetles, compound progression for wolves. 系统化命名族群——关联生物共享后缀或规律，使玩家能直觉地理解关系：蜜蜂用-by、甲虫用-eetle、狼系用复合词递进。
  • Avoiding competitor-franchise conventions — No "-type" element suffixes, no "balls" for capture devices, no "shiny" for rare variants. The game maintains its own identity. 规避竞品惯例——不使用"-type"元素后缀、不用"ball"指代捕获装置、不用"shiny"指代稀有变体。游戏保持自身独特性。
  • Brand-aware coinage — Key terms (Aniimo, Aniipod, Twine) are original coinages that are searchable, trademarkable and distinctive — essential for a game building its own IP. 品牌意识造词——核心术语（Aniimo、Aniipod、Twine）为原创新词，具有可搜索性、可注册商标性和辨识度——对构建自有IP的游戏至关重要。
